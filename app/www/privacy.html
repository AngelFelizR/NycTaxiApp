Privacy notice

Last updated: 6 October 2026

NYC Taxi Decision Simulator is a demonstration: you play a simulated eight-hour shift as an NYC taxi driver, and the result can be shared. This page explains what that stores, why, for how long, and how to have it removed. It is deliberately short; if something here is unclear, write to us and we will explain it.

What we store

  • Your decisions in the simulation — which rides you accepted or rejected, and where. These are the game itself and are never linked to anything that identifies you.
  • Your email address — only if you type one. It is optional at every step and there is no way to play without one.
  • Your name — only if you give it, and only for the same purposes as the email.
  • A hash of your IP address — SHA-256 of your address mixed with a secret salt we keep. We use it to limit abuse (a few sign-ups per day per connection). The plain IP address is never written down, not in the database, not in Redis, not in logs.
  • Whether you consented to receiving your result card and to being contacted about Data Science services, as two separate flags.

Why we store it

  • To let you resume a day you started.
  • To send you your result card if you asked for it.
  • To contact you about Data Science services — only if you ticked that box, which starts unticked and is entirely optional. Saying no costs you nothing in the product.
  • To keep the demo fair: rate limits, and aggregated statistics such as how a simulated day compares with the model.

We do not sell data, we do not run advertising, and there are no third-party analytics or tracking pixels on this site.

Cookies and local storage

There are no tracking cookies. The site uses localStorage for two working features: the resume code that gets you back into an unfinished day, and remembering that you have already seen the introduction. Clearing your browser storage removes both, and only those.

How long we keep it

  • Simulations and decisions are kept indefinitely once they no longer point at anybody, because their value is aggregate.
  • Email and name are kept until you ask us to delete them.
  • Backups are rotated every 28 days, so a deletion reaches every copy within a month at most.
  • Waitlist addresses stay until the list is served or you ask us to remove them.

Who touches it

The application runs on our own server; your result card is rendered behind Cloudflare, which sees your request as any content delivery network does. If you choose to share your result on X or LinkedIn, that happens in your browser and their terms apply to the link you post. Emails are sent through a transactional mail provider, which sees your address and nothing else.

Ask us to delete your data

Write to angel.esteban.feliz@gmail.com from the address you used, or quote the address you used, with “Data deletion request” in the subject. We clear your email, name and marketing consent, and remove you from the waitlist if you are on it. The simulations stay, anonymised. We aim to answer within 30 days and will confirm when it is done.
